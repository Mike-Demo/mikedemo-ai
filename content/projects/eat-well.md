# Eat Well

A dietary restaurant finder for the Pimoroni Tufty 2350 badge: 557 vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, kosher, and halal spots across 14 US cities, with MapQuest QR codes on every detail screen.

## About

Eat Well is a dietary restaurant finder built for the Pimoroni Tufty 2350 (Badger/Badgeware) and entered in the SELECT Badge Contest. Pick a city, filter by diet, browse results, and open a detail view with cuisine, price, rating, neighborhood, and notes — each with a precomputed QR code that opens the restaurant in MapQuest. The 557-listing dataset spans 14 US metros, with an AI-powered web companion (Qwen3 running in-browser via WebGPU) for natural-language search. Built for a friend who keeps kosher and a vegan co-diner.
