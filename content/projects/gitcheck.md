# GitCheck

Scan a public repo's dependency licenses, map the whole chain, and see exactly where they clash.

## About

GitCheck scans a public GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repo for open-source license compatibility: it detects the project's own license (SPDX), parses dependency manifests, resolves direct and transitive dependencies, maps the license chain, and highlights the risks. Built on bolt.new Forge.
