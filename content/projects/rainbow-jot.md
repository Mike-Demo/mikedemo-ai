# Rainbow Jot

A pride-themed idea board for capturing, arranging, and voting on a room’s thoughts.

## About

A colorful, session-only idea board built for group ideation. Add and edit sticky notes, drag them into clusters, vote with hearts, invite participants by QR code, switch to a full-screen presentation view, and clear the board when the session ends. Ideas stay in the browser session and are not saved.
