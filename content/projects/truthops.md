# TruthOps

Don't just detect misinformation. Resolve it — a transparent verification pipeline with a human approval gate.

## About

TruthOps is a transparent, rule-based misinformation verification pipeline: it scores high-risk claims, runs a scripted contrarian check and photo forensics over curated evidence, and produces evidence-backed response plans behind a human approval gate. Built for neatHack 2026.
