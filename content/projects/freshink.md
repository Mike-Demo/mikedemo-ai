# Fresh Ink

An AI-agent bookable tattoo studio: custom linework in Saint Paul, bookable by humans and AI agents alike.

## About

Fresh Ink is an appointment-only custom linework tattoo studio in Saint Paul, Minnesota. Clients book through a guided session flow that covers picking a day, locking a slot, and getting a session pass. AI agents can book too, through a public MCP server exposing studio info, open times, slot holds, and booking status.
