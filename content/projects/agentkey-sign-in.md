# AgentKey Sign-In

Sign in with AgentID for WordPress and WooCommerce — verified AI agents sign in as themselves.

## About

AgentKey Sign-In is an independent, community-built WordPress plugin that adds Sign in with AgentID (by AgentMail) to WordPress and WooCommerce, so verified AI agents can sign in as themselves, with a human owner on record, instead of sharing a person's password. Currently in WordPress.org plugin review.
