# QueerCade Connect

A curated arcade of games with LGBTQ+ characters and stories, built for discovery and personal play tracking.

## About

QueerCade Connect is a curated arcade for discovering games with LGBTQ+ characters and stories. Players can browse featured picks, search and filter the full library, explore human-curated collections, and track games they are playing, completed, wishlisting, or dropping. Its editorial workflow combines IGDB source data with content curated in Sanity.
